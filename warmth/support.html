Frequently asked

Warmth doesn't have a Dock icon — where did it go?
Warmth lives in the menu bar only, by design. Look for its icon near your clock.

Colors look off even with Warmth disabled.
Night Shift and True Tone (System Settings → Displays) apply their own tint on top of Warmth's. Turn them off if you want Warmth to be the only thing adjusting color.

What macOS version does Warmth need?
macOS 15.0 or later.

Does Warmth need location access?
Only if you want sunrise/sunset-based scheduling. You can set a fixed location manually instead in Settings → Location. See the privacy policy for details.
